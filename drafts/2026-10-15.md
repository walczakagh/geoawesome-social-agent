**What it shows:** A preview of 10 Years, 10 Adventures, a set of ten animated 3D travel maps made by the travel blog Trois Pas de Côté (Three Steps Off the Coast) to mark ten years of blogging. The maps run on a bespoke WebGL engine with custom heightmap and route data rather than Mapbox or Google Maps SDKs, and include narrated 3D camera flights synced to an interactive elevation profile. Trips include Martinique, Iceland, the Isle of Skye and the Canadian Rockies.
