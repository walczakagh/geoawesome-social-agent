**Source:** [NASA Earth Observatory](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/vega-island-frozen-in-fright/) · score 8.0/10 · Striking Antarctic satellite image of Vega Island's skull-like ice; NASA EO history strong.
**Rights:** Publisher allows reuse with credit. Licence on page: NASA imagery, generally free to reuse with credit.

**What it shows:** Two Landsat 9 OLI images of Vega Island, east of the Antarctic Peninsula in the Weddell Sea, where ice, rock outcrops and glacial moraines look like a smirking skull in profile facing Devil Island (a nunatak forms the eye socket, a lateral moraine the mouth, Devil's Bay Glacier the jawbone). In the square, top is 22 March 2025, with a layer of snow over the ice, and bottom is 10 October 2026, with snow absent from large areas and darker bare ice exposed. Glaciologist Mauri Pelto attributes the bare ice partly to above-average winter temperatures in 2026, particularly in June.
