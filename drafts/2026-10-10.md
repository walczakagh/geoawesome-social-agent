## 1. Gibraltar imaged by IRIDE Eaglet II

**What it shows:** An early commissioning image of Gibraltar from one of the IRIDE Eaglet II satellites launched before the final batch of eight. The constellation was built by OHB Italia, and the image was received at OHB Italia's Control Centre in Rome.
