**What it shows:** A view from Sliding Shores, an interactive 3D globe that redraws coastlines for preset sea level scenarios, here showing North America after a theoretical sea level rise of about 690 metres. The tool has 16 preset scenarios, from draining the oceans entirely (-11,000 m) and the Last Ice Age coastline (-120 m) to total ice sheet melt (+70 m) and drowning Mt. Everest (+8,849 m). Maps Mania stresses that the 690 m scenario is purely theoretical, as melting every ice sheet would raise sea level by only around 80 m.
